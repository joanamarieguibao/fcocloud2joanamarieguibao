2026 Timeline

FOUR PHASES
PHASE 01

January — May

Preparation for the year included studio sessions, choreography, the release of the Signals EP, Coachella appearances, Billboard's Women in Music event, and the Kaogma People Festival.

PHASE 02

June — July

BINI officially kicked off the global tour in the Philippines with stadium shows in Manila and Cebu before making an international appearance at San Diego Comic-Con.

PHASE 03

August — September

The tour expanded across North America, Japan, Australia, and New Zealand, including BINI's appearances at Summer Sonic Festival in Osaka and Tokyo.

PHASE 04

October — November

The final phase brings the Signals World Tour through Asia, including Singapore and New Taipei City.
